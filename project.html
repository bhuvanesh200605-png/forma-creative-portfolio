One idea.

A warm, produce-inspired palette, confident type, and playful circular forms create a flexible system that can scale from a poster to a small digital tile.

  • Build a compact, reusable color and type system
  • Keep key messages readable at different sizes
  • Adapt the design across campaign touchpoints
